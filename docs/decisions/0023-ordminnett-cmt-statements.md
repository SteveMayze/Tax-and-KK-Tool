# ADR 0023: Ord Minnett Cash Management Trust statements

Status: accepted (Phase 23, 2026-10-20; owner answers: withholding is paired with the interest of the same quarter and its debit date goes in the comment; property-sale credits are not income, shown as a notice; stock sales are out of scope, a notice only; payment date = credit date).

## Context
24 statements (22 monthly, July 2024 to May 2025 and July 2025 to May 2026, and two financial-year statements that replace June and repeat earlier months) plus one holdings snapshot. The table has one amount column and a running balance (Brought Forward row, no closing row, dates "dd Mon yy"). Interest and "Overseas Withholding Tax" appear on the quarter-end dates, withholding right after the interest. Direct credits are mostly dividends from companies (already covered by the share advices), plus tax-office refunds, share-scheme consideration and one large non-dividend credit; debits are BPAY, direct debits and transfers (one stock sale is visible only as a proceeds transfer). The financial-year statements end with a taxation summary per quarter.

## Decisions
1. **Reader `ordminnett_cmt`** (layout mode) recognised by structure; `ordminnett_valuation` recognises the holdings snapshot and returns nothing to import. The Australian dividend reader no longer claims files with a "Balance ($)" column or a "Portfolio Valuation": before this phase it claimed 23 of the 25 files and refused them.
2. **Direction from the running balance only**, proved per row (O1); unprovable rows refuse the statement.
3. **Rows are made in a batch step (`settle`)**, since the same interest line can appear in a monthly and a financial-year statement and an FY-only quarter exists nowhere else: events (interest, withholding) are collected per statement, exact repeats across statements are dropped (first by file name wins, the repeats are listed), withholding is paired with the interest of the same calendar quarter, and the row is added to the statement that holds the interest. Two identical lines inside one statement are not repeats.
4. **Pairing is by calendar quarter** (the samples have both lines on the quarter-end date; a debit dated in the next quarter would not pair and gives a warning). An orphan withholding is a warning and no row (O10); an interest credit without a debit stages with withholding 0 and a warning.
5. **Classification** by booking text: interest, overseas withholding tax, direct credit (dividend, refund or other: not staged), BPAY, direct debit, transfers, own-account transfers (BSB pattern); notices (date only) for scheme consideration, stock-sale proceeds and large credits (>= AUD 20,000 without a dividend reference). The large-credit threshold is a heuristic that only controls a notice. Anything else refuses the statement naming its first words.
6. **Taxation summary** of the FY statements: quarterly gross interest and non-resident withholding compared with the lines; warnings only. In the samples both summaries agree exactly.
7. **Staging order changed:** the staging now classifies files (staged / nothing to import / noticed) after the readers' batch steps, so rows created by a batch step are staged; the inventory tool runs the same step.
8. **Layout failure no longer hides a file from plain-text readers** (see Consequences).

## Consequences
All 25 files read: 8 interest rows (the quarters Sep 2024, Dec 2024, Mar 2025, Jun 2025, Sep 2025, Dec 2025, Mar 2026, Jun 2026), 17 files with nothing to import, notices for the scheme credit, the stock-sale transfer and the large credit, 12 repeated lines staged once; every row has a withholding debit and both taxation summaries agree. The owner's checked rows go in the git-ignored `local/samples/expected/ords.csv`; collisions with rows already in the database are Phase 24. Sales remain unrecorded by decision (rules README item 19).

**Regression found and fixed in this phase (introduced in Phase 16).** `statement_readers.read_statement` returned the "no text layer" error for the whole file whenever layout extraction came back empty. Once a reader that needs layout was registered (HSBC, Phase 16), 36 of the 76 Australian dividend advices in `local/samples/Securities` (whose layout text is empty) were refused as scans. The Phase 15 identity check on the real samples had been run only once. Layout failure now means "layout readers do not claim the file"; a test covers it and the 87 Phase 14 samples again give identical rows. The earlier phases' tests had not caught it because their synthetic PDFs always have a layout text.
