# ADR 0036: Tax information summary rebuilt as income as recorded, with an original-currency filter

Status: accepted (Phase 40, 2026-11-04). Reporting change only: no model, migration, import or stored payment changed; nothing is stored. ADR 0035 stays reserved for the storage work (`PHASES_10.md`). The German wording of the new headings is proposed by Claude and awaits the owner's review (`docs/glossary.md` section E).

## Why
The tax adviser could not work with the 2025 report. It grouped payments by the category's `tax_income_type` and by an inferred country (`effective_country`), so a label such as "Other income, e.g. pensions (section 22)" held payments the owner does not regard as pensions, interest and dividends were not shown under their own names, German income could not be left out, withholding tax was one EUR column, and nothing tied a figure to its source. The report tried to interpret German tax law, which the adviser does, not the tool.

## Decision
- **Same report key, URL, title, CSV and PDF routes, notice and signature block (ADR 0034).** Only the content changes. Four titled tables, all computed in `core/services/tax_income_report.py` (Decimal, ORM, no raw SQL): (1) income by stored category and original currency, with a EUR-only subtotal per category; (2) income by category, organisation and currency; (3) taxes withheld (only payments with a non-zero recorded withholding, a total per currency and in EUR); (4) every payment as stored (date, category, organisation, currency, gross, withholding, rate, rate date, rate source, EUR amounts, kind, comment).
- **Only stored fields are read:** category name, organisation name, original currency, amounts, rate fields and kind. Never `tax_income_type`, `sbk_treatment`, the organisation country or the inferred country, and no "section" or "Anlage" wording. A test changes those fields and shows the report does not move.
- **No interpretation.** Original-currency amounts of different currencies are never added. EUR figures are the sums of the stored EUR amounts; unconverted payments are listed, counted and left out of EUR. "Of which negative" counts normal negative payments only; corrections and reversals are listed with their kind. Nothing is netted, credited or classified. Active categories without payments in the selection are named in one basis line.
- **Smallest extension for several tables:** `reports.Table` and the optional `Report.tables` (`Report.csv_tables` names the tables the CSV carries). A report with no tables behaves exactly as before; every other report's HTML, CSV and PDF text was compared with `main` over a synthetic dataset and is identical (the only difference is the tax summary's one-line description on the reports index). The PDF layer receives the finished display cells and computes nothing.
- **Currency filter:** `ReportFilter.currency_codes` and `currency_mode` (`include` or `exclude`), applied once in `ReportFilter.apply` by the payment's ORIGINAL currency, combined with organisation and category by AND. Codes are upper-cased, checked against `SUPPORTED_CURRENCIES`, repeated codes collapse, and errors are an error message and redirect in the style of the existing filter errors (at most 10 characters echoed). Nothing ticked is no restriction in either mode. The `Filter` basis row (also in the CSV and PDF) states the codes, the mode and "Filtered by the payment's original currency, not by country or source". The form group (check boxes plus two radio buttons, default "Show only the ticked currencies") is shown on this report only (`ReportSpec.currency_filter`); the shared parser and `apply` honour the parameter wherever the filter is used (for example the payments page), but `options(..., currencies=True)` (one extra query) is only asked for by this report.
- **CSV:** header block with the basis and `Filter` rows, then the payment table, then the category-by-currency table, each introduced by a `Table` row. `CSV_FORMAT_VERSION` is one global constant and stays `2` (owner's decision): this report's layout is new under the same number, and every other CSV is byte-identical.

## Not done / left to later phases
Source references and the document index (Phase 41), the package with the original statements (Phase 42). Expenses and net amounts are not shown (rental costs are deferred). The policy code (`policies/tax.py`: `tax_summary_rows`, `effective_country`, `POLICY_VERSION`, `NOT_APPLIED`) stays: the tax checks and their tests use it, and the old income-type grouping is simply no longer reachable from the reports.

## Consequences
The "Categories without payments" line prints stored category names, including ones such as a pension category, as data; it is not a label of any figure. The old report's tests were updated where they asserted its table (listed in the Phase 40 report).
